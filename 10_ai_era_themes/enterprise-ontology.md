# Enterprise Data Ontology

Enterprise data ontology is a semantic model that defines concepts in a specific domain, their attributes, and relationships between them, encoding them in a graphical format that is readable both by computers and humans.

It represents a generalized model describing the **types** of entities and relationships existing in a domain, rather than specific entities in that domain.

Example: an online store can model its domain as an ontology with entities like "Customer", "Product", "Order"; attributes such as name, phone number, quantity, product ID; and relationships such as "Places" and "Contains".
