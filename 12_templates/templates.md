# DG Program Guide — Workshop Templates Catalog

The board packages its methods as reusable workshop templates, each following a standard convention: a cover frame (colored sidebar, goal statement, "Relevant links", "Back to the board" navigation), a filled example frame demonstrating the method, and a blank working copy for participants. Several templates also link out to Excel/Google Sheets versions for use outside Miro.

---

## Pains Analysis (Analysis of Data-related Pains in the Company)
**Goal:** Identify and analyze data/analytics pain points across organizational roles so solution design targets specific problems (framed as preferable to an economic-model debate with management).
**Structure:** A grid of color-coded, role-specific pain zones — entire company, "casual" users, top management, BI team, power users/analysts, data platform/DWH team — around a central vertical "Cross-Roles Pains" column. Two reference images show filled example boards.
**How to run:** Workshop facilitation: every role group posts stickies into its zone ("let everyone speak up about their pains"), cross-cutting pains go to the central column.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453561752
**External file:** none

## Problems → Solutions (→ Initiatives) Canvas
**Goal:** Decompose one concrete data pain into a problems tree, map solutions, and package them into initiatives.
**Structure:** Two variants. Simple: two-column Problems | Solutions canvas anchored to the example "Data quality is insufficient for launching the deal forecasting model" (two near-duplicate copies). Extended: problems tree (yellow stickies, solid arrows) → solutions grid (green stickies, dotted mapping arrows) → three dashed initiative containers bundling solutions, demonstrated on a supply-chain digital-transformation example.
**How to run:** Left to right — decompose the root problem, map each problem node to solutions, then bundle selected solutions into 2-3 initiatives.

**External file:** none

## Data Domain Classifier of the Company
**Goal:** Identify and classify data subject areas (domains/subdomains) and assign ownership/stewardship roles for future management.
**Structure:** Two side-by-side 13x5 registry grids ("Register data domains"): a filled example (Finance and HR domains with subdomains) and a blank working copy. Columns: Data Domain | Subdomain | Data Owner | Business Data Steward | Tech Data Steward / Data Custodian.
**How to run:** Fill the blank grid with your own domains; role columns are intentionally left blank even in the example — assigning them is the exercise.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453561758
**External file:** none

## Domain / Subdomain Map
**Goal:** Visually map the company's data domains, subdomains, and cross-domain relationships (DDD-style / enterprise-architecture mapping).
**Structure:** Large color-coded hexagons per domain with color-matched subdomain stickies, small gray peripheral hexagons for future domains, and ~10 pre-drawn dotted cross-domain connectors. Filled example: Sales, Finance, Operations, Marketing, HR, Services Delivery (e.g. Sales-Operations linked via Client Master Data); blank counterpart has 7 generic domains with empty stickies.
**How to run:** Name the hexagons, fill subdomain stickies, then draw/label connectors where subdomains are shared across domains.

**External file:** none

## Data Mesh Canvas
**Goal:** Classify the company's data products by archetype and map cross-domain consumption dependencies.
**Structure:** Three horizontal swim lanes — Source-aligned (bottom), Aggregate (middle), Consumer-aligned (top) — with a legend of dotted "Domain Team" groupings containing "Data Product" boxes. Worked example places 11 data products (Search Queries, Orders, 360° Customer, Profitability Reporting, ...) with 15 dashed consumption connectors. Based on datamesh-architecture.com.
**How to run:** Place each data product in its lane under its owning domain team, then draw consumption dependencies upward.
**Board link:** template https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453561762 ; example ...moveToWidget=3458764611453561761
**External file:** https://www.datamesh-architecture.com/datamesh-canvas (source)

## DG Vision Statement
**Goal:** Author a clear, inspiring Data Governance / D&A vision defining future direction, key initiatives, and core metrics.
**Structure:** Three main sections: Vision Statement (with a grayed-out sample bank vision — data monetization, CDO leadership, key assets); Strategic Goals and Strategic Initiatives tables (columns: KPIs/Metrics | Business Outcomes | Timing); Challenges & Risks with Risk Reduction Factors. A right-hand panel gives an 11-question guidance checklist (value extraction, critical assets, ownership, stakeholders, alignment, services, objectives, products, KPIs, risks, priorities).
**How to run:** Work through the 11-question checklist to draft the vision, then fill the goals/initiatives/risks tables.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453561764
**External file:** Excel/Google Sheets version linked on the frame (exact URL not extracted)

## DG Goals Configurator

## DG Scope Configurator
**Goal:** Configure the scope of a DG program by mapping selected goals to initiative streams, success factors, and value drivers.
**Structure:** A mapping canvas: Data Governance Goals (20 stickies) → DG Initiative streams (10 streams with sticky sub-initiatives: DQ management system, metadata management, security & compliance, data classification, data platform development, roles model & operating framework, data standards, golden layer, data culture, analytics governance) → Most common Success factors (responsibility taken, executive sponsorship, business experts engaged, use-case registry, service SLAs) → DG Value drivers.
**How to run:** Define the most important goals and remove the rest; check the linked DG streams that serve those goals; identify success factors; remove everything else.

## Data Catalog Implementation Requirements (Brainstorming)
**Goal:** Structure the requirements for a data catalog implementation — key features, value, selection criteria, risks, and rollout priorities.
**Structure:** Five exercise blocks: (1) Core functions (pre-populated primary/secondary/tertiary feature stickies), (2) Primary value, (3) Selection factors, (4) Top challenges, (5) Implementation sequence on a timeline with pilot group → MVP release → product release milestones.
**How to run:** Brainstorm-and-vote per block; the canvas comes pre-populated with example stickies to react to rather than start from blank.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453561780
**External file:** none

## Data-Driven Index (DDI)
**Goal:** Measure the organization's data maturity as a single weighted composite score: DD Index = Σ(weights × Component).
**Structure:** A 26-row scoring grid — component / example metric / calculation complexity / formula / current value / weight / weighted contribution — over 13 components (Data Literacy 0.15, Tools Adoption 0.15, Direct Business Value 0.13, ...; weights sum to 1.00; example total 62.99). A companion dashboard mockup shows the index card (62.9, +11.1 YoY) and the Data Literacy L1-L4 scoring detail.
**How to run:** Tailor the component and metric list to what your company can effectively calculate and what is meaningful for the business; recompute periodically.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611455329213 ; dashboard mockup ...moveToWidget=3458764611455329214
**External file:** none

## Healthcare DG Business Case (Jeff Fuller, Divurgent)
**Goal:** A customizable business case and executive guide for establishing a formal (healthcare) DG program — reusable as a business-case skeleton for any industry.
**Structure:** Part One is educational/persuasive (DG definition, role in analytics/AI, executive talking points, barriers to adoption); Part Two is a 10-section fill-in template with a 3-phase plan (Foundation / Launch / Engagement), resourcing guidance (1-3 FTE DG Office, existing tools before buying a catalog), and an illustrative cost-benefit framework (tech ~$2-6K; personnel ~$115-250K; advisory ~$200-400K; ROI incl. >50% less time locating data).
**How to run:** Fill the 10 sections (stakeholders, objective, current state, drivers, components, benefits, plan, resourcing, cost-benefit, approval request) and adapt talking points for your executives.
**Board link:** https://miro.com/app/board/uXjVMBRtQEA=/?moveToWidget=3458764611453525298
**External file:** embedded document on the frame (no external URL)
