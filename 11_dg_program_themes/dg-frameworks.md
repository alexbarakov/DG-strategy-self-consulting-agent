# Data Governance Frameworks

## What the board teaches
A DG framework is defined (per the Data Governance Institute) as "a logical structure for classifying, organizing, and communicating the complex activities associated with enterprise data decision-making and action-taking" — the conceptual blueprint of a program, explicitly *not yet* an implementation project. The board surveys seven reference frameworks — DGI's ten-component model (rules / people / processes, with Goals-Metrics-Funding, Controls, and the DG Office highlighted), PwC's four-layer Enterprise Data Governance Framework, the DAMA Wheel and DAMA Pyramid (Peter Aiken), the Eckerson Group's six-layer people-centric framework ("we don't govern the data, we govern what people do when working with data"), Informatica's framework, and Seiner's Non-Invasive Data Governance. Instead of adopting one wholesale, the recommended recipe is: (1) study several existing frameworks, (2) draft your focus areas — core elements, org-specific priorities, and explicit exclusions, (3) sketch your own framework with the team in a half-day session. The "Your DG Framework" mind map then shows what a company-level framework must define: from key terms and program goals through role model, cost-benefit model, policy, tech stack, and communications — with DG Vision as input and Funding/Executive Support as the enabling condition.

## Key objects
- DG framework definition (DGI quote); caveat "abstract model, not an implementation project"
- DGI Data Governance Framework: 10 universal components (why-what-who-how) grouped into rules, people, processes; Goals/Metrics/Funding; preventive/corrective Controls; Data Governance Office (DGO)
- PwC Enterprise Data Governance Framework (from "Demystifying Data Governance", Nov 2019): Data Management; Enablers (People & Culture, Process & Operating Model, Tools & Technology); Stewardship & Core Layer (lineage, glossary, master data, metadata, quality & trust, access & ownership, privacy & security); Governance Strategy & Vision (Control/Efficiency vs Growth/Monetization)
- Other referenced models: DAMA Wheel (DG at the center of knowledge areas), DAMA Pyramid (Aiken), Eckerson Group (6 layers, 39 components), Informatica, Non-Invasive DG (Seiner), Willem Koenders' analogy
- 3-step "build your own framework" recipe
- "Your DG Framework" mind map — 10 branches: key DG terms; program goals (value drivers, pain points, effectiveness metrics); data prioritization (domain classification, access rights matrix); operational management model (roles, governance bodies, monitoring); cost-benefit model; DG processes and responsibility matrix; high-level DG policy; priority initiatives; DG technology stack (catalog, DQ tool); communication and marketing system
- Root chain: DG Vision → Your DG Framework → Funding and Executive Support
- A dedicated DG framework workshop template exists — see [templates.md](../12_templates/templates.md)
