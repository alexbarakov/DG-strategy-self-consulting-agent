## Summary
Research findings on how DG programs get started and sponsored. DG is still transitioning from informal to structured: in 40% of companies DG is not a separate program/team but is integrated into data platform processes, and most dedicated programs started within the last three years. Programs are commonly justified via a data catalog and DQ framework; "pure DG" is recognized only when distrust in reporting becomes critical. Top-down starts are easiest where a CDO exists (45% of companies; sponsors also CTO/CEO); bottom-up progress comes from infosec (30%) and mature domain teams — success needs both.

## Objects
- Finding: 40% — DG not a separate program, embedded in data platform processes
- Finding: launch via data catalog + DQ framework justification; reporting distrust as trigger
- Finding: 45% have CDO (top-down easiest); 30% bottom-up via infosec; both directions required
- Pull quote: TTM effect tracking generally not done; final driver is the will of the data platform head — "a leap of faith"
- Annotation: a mature data platform knows what should happen at each stage and handles tasks automatically
