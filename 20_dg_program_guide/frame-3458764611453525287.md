## Summary
A template roadmap for rolling out a DG program across 4 stages (each roughly half a year). A 13x5 grid maps 12 DG workstreams plus "Other initiatives" against Stage 1-4, with 61 color-coded task cards placed in the cells; card width indicates tasks spanning multiple stages. Recurring checkpoint cards ("Refining the goals of the program and the roadmap", "Refining the data domain structure, role model, and processes + audit of critical assets") appear in multiple stages. Explicitly labeled "possible" — a reference template, not a fixed plan.
