## Summary
A self-assessment checklist of 13 statements (effectively 12 unique — #1 and #3 are duplicates) about common organizational data pain points. A company checks which statements apply to it; if at least 5 of the 12 are relevant, implementing a Data Governance program is likely to be beneficial and cost-effective. The scoring threshold is highlighted in green as the key decision criterion.

## Objects
- Title: "Test to Determine the Need for a Data Governance Program"
- 13 numbered statements, covering:
  - Cross-functional analytical reports from various systems (duplicated as #1 and #3)
  - Multiple data sources needing unified, accurate access
  - Confidential/personal information requiring security and protection
  - Poor data quality impeding decisions, causing errors and management distrust
  - Third-party data requiring quality/reliability assurance
  - Regulated market with changing data protection / privacy legislation
  - Many analysts needing to find relevant datasets in a data platform
  - Multiple applications requiring data consistency and integrity
  - Competitive market with rapidly changing digital products
  - Data duplication and misinterpretation across systems
  - Large data volumes requiring cost-effective storage and management
  - Product/service improvement projects requiring high-quality data
- Green-highlighted scoring callout: threshold "5 of 12 statements" → DG program is warranted
