## Summary
An educational frame defining Data Products: prepared, non-raw data ready for wide consumer use, with trust as the core characteristic. It lists requirements a data product must meet (labeled/described/interpretable data, guaranteed quality with transparent issue communication, change notifications to consumers, purpose-driven design with captured consumer requirements, accessibility/discoverability, interoperability with connection/auth/download). Includes comparative diagrams (complex vs efficient data product approaches, components, archetypes) and recommends a simple register (spreadsheet) as a practical starting point, with a Google Sheets template link.
