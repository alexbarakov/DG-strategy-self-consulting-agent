## Summary
Template for registering and classifying a company's data domains and subdomains, and assigning ownership/stewardship roles. Goal: "To identify and classify different data subject areas within the organization for more effective management in the future." Contains two side-by-side 13x5 registry grids under "Register data domains" headers: one pre-filled example (Finance and HR domains with subdomains) and one blank working copy. Columns encode the DG role model: Data Owner, Business Data Steward, and Tech Data Steward / Data Custodian per subdomain.

## Objects
- Goal statement and left teal instruction panel with "Useful links" and "Back to the board"
- Example registry grid: Finance domain (Finance results, P&L, Cost allocation, Planning & forecasting, ...) and HR domain (Salary data, Terminations and attrition, Trainings, Vacation, Headcount structure, ...)
- Blank working grid with identical columns for the user's own domains
- Column schema: Data Domain | Subdomain | Data Owner | Business Data Steward | Tech Data Steward / Data Custodian
- "..." rows signaling the lists are expandable; role columns intentionally left blank even in the example
