## Summary
One option in a series of BI/analytics organizational design models: a hybrid combining a centralized BI Center of Excellence with an embedded cross-functional analytics team. The org chart shows CAO/COO and CPO at the top, a Data & Analytics Lead / Head of Cross-functional Analytics, a BI CoE / Community Leader, and data/business analysts, plus a separate "low data literate domain" branch with its own analytic leads. A 13-criterion evaluation panel rates the model: strong on governance, security, one-version-of-truth, cross-functional reporting, navigation/UX, dedup of efforts, business alignment, request throughput, TTM, and high-context insights; challenging (amber) on cost-effective capacity balancing, quality/standardisation, and people development/retention/rotation.

## Objects
- Org chart: CAO/COO, CPO -> D&A Lead / Head of Cross-functional Analytics -> BI CoE / Community Leader -> Data Analyst + Business Analyst; separate low-data-literacy domain branch (Data Analytic Lead -> Data Analyst x2)
- Dotted boundary marking the cross-functional BI/Analytics team scope
- Evaluation panel "How easy is to provide:" — 13 criteria with 3-square ratings; amber flags: cost-effective capacity balancing, standardisation/quality, people development and retention
