## Summary
Only 2 of 20 companies have a consolidated glossary and metrics tree. Metric misalignment is a recognized pain, yet glossary initiatives are consistently postponed — lacking skills to engage business experts and facilitate cross-domain collaboration; data stewards and a parallel semantic layer project (dbt-metrics-like) are prerequisites. Glossaries occasionally emerge in mature domains but rarely get elevated company-wide. The author's editorial counterpoint: companies have run for years on shared concepts without formal glossaries; for data teams the physical layer, metric store, and golden layer are more critical.

## Objects
- Stat: 2/20 companies with consolidated glossary + metrics tree
- Finding: postponement due to missing steward skills and cross-domain facilitation; semantic layer needed in parallel
- Finding: domain-level glossaries rarely scaled company-wide
- Editorial annotation: physical layer / metric store / golden layer more critical than the glossary
