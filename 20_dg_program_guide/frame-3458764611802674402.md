## Summary
Part of the author's **"My thoughts 🧙‍♂️"** conclusions block in the DG Kitchen research deck; the section heading (highlighted lilac) reads **"Land and expand with a DG program"**. The frame is a dependency graph of 20 initiative cards laid out left to right, starting from a single MVP case, plus a two-part legend, a budget-failure loop and a bottom row of out-of-chain projects.

## Objects

### Legend (top of the frame)
Card colour = stage: blue `#2d9bf0` = **Stage 1 – "Essential Minimum"**; purple `#9510ac` = **Stage 2 – "Reasonable Addition"**; orange `#ff9e4a` = **Stage 3 – "Matured Focus"**. A second legend block shows a sample card ("Creation of the Semantic Layer and Metric Store") annotated with the four attributes every card is meant to carry: **Leading Role**, **Secondary Role**, **Tooling**, **Initiative Effort**.

### Entry point and the failure loop
Left edge, label **"MVP Case"**, pointing at the first card: *"Complete analysis of the company's data pain points, design of the DG program concept, and buy-in from sponsors"* (Stage 1). Two grey annotations feed it: **"Business Domain Owner from the MVP"**, itself fed by **"Support from the CDO, CTO, CFO, CEO"**; and **"Interest from Other Business Domain Owners"**. A crimson dashed line leaves the same card for a small crimson circle labelled **"Budget Denial"**, annotated **"Ongoing Development of Common Sense DG; Restart MVP"** — if the budget is refused you fall back to common-sense DG and restart the MVP rather than stopping.

### The dependency graph (arrows as drawn)
From the MVP-case card three branches start: → *Metrics Tree and Business Ontology* (S1); → *Design of Domain Structure* (S1); → *Standardization of Data Lifecycle Operations on the Platform (Data/BIOps)* (S1).
- *Metrics Tree and Business Ontology* → *Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance* (S1); → *Development and Maintenance of the Glossary* (S3).
- *Design of Domain Structure* → *Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance*; → *Assigning Owners and Other Roles to Objects* (S1).
- *Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance* → *Development and Maintenance of the Glossary* (S3); → *Classification of PII Data* (S2); → *Creation and Development of the Golden Data Layer* (S2).
- *Creation and Development of the Golden Data Layer* → *Creation of the Semantic Layer and Metric Store* (S3).
- *Classification of PII Data* → *Automation of Data and Data Product Access Based on Roles and Domains* (S3).
- *Assigning Owners and Other Roles to Objects* → *Setting Up Documentation Management Based on Criticality* (S1); → *DQM – Coverage with Checks Based on Criticality / Public DQ Status* (S1).

- *Incident Management and Data Quality Improvement* → *Implementation of Data Contracts* (S2).

### Bottom row — "+100500 of other good projects"
Five cards sit outside the dependency chain: *Reporting on DWH/BI, Metric Monitoring* (S1); *Aligning Team Priorities* (S1); *Architectural Committee, DG Committee* (S2); *Sponsorship management, accumulation of cases, promotion* (S2); *Selection and Development of Tools: DQ tools, Data catalog tools, dbt-like metric tools, data contracts engines, data observability tools, etc.* (S3). The label to their right reads "+100500 of other good projects".

### Stage assignment summary
- **Stage 1 "Essential Minimum"**: MVP pain-point analysis and sponsor buy-in; Metrics Tree and Business Ontology; Design of Domain Structure; Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance; Assigning Owners and Other Roles to Objects; Setting Up Documentation Management Based on Criticality; Standardization of Data Lifecycle Operations (Data/BIOps); DQM coverage with checks + public DQ status; Incident Management and DQ Improvement; Reporting on DWH/BI and metric monitoring; Aligning Team Priorities.
- **Stage 2 "Reasonable Addition"**: Classification of PII Data; Creation and Development of the Golden Data Layer; Implementation of Data Contracts (embedded quality and documentation as code); Architectural Committee / DG Committee; Sponsorship management, accumulation of cases, promotion.
- **Stage 3 "Matured Focus"**: Development and Maintenance of the Glossary; Creation of the Semantic Layer and Metric Store; Automation of Data and Data Product Access Based on Roles and Domains; Selection and Development of Tools.
