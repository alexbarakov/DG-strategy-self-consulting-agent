## Summary
A "land and expand" DG program roadmap mapping initiatives across three maturity stages: Stage 1 "Essential Minimum" (blue), Stage 2 "Reasonable Addition" (purple), Stage 3 "Matured Focus" (orange). The flow starts from an MVP (pain-point analysis, DG concept design, sponsor buy-in) and branches into initiative cards with dependencies; a Budget Denial failure path loops back to "Ongoing Development of Common Sense DG; Restart MVP". Sponsorship (CDO/CTO/CFO/CEO) is a precondition to scaling beyond MVP; a bottom row lists parallel ongoing activities supporting the roadmap.

## Objects
- Stage 1 (Essential Minimum): MVP; design of domain structure; metrics tree and business ontology; standardization of data lifecycle operations; classification of metrics/data objects by importance; assigning owners and roles; documentation management by criticality; incident management and DQ improvement; DQM coverage by criticality / public DQ status
- Stage 2 (Reasonable Addition): golden data layer; PII classification; data contracts
- Stage 3 (Matured Focus): glossary development and maintenance; automation of data/data-product access; semantic layer and metric store
- Failure path: Budget Denial -> Common Sense DG + restart MVP
- Expansion triggers: business domain owner from the MVP; interest from other domain owners; CDO/CTO/CFO/CEO support
- Bottom row (ongoing): reporting on DWH/BI and metric monitoring; aligning team priorities; architectural/DG committee; sponsorship management; tool selection; "+100500 other good projects"
- Legend: leading vs secondary role; tooling vs initiative effort
