## Summary
Scorecard sub-frame for the "3. Federated analytics" data team operating model (part of the Data Teams Modeling template, frame 3458764611929480210). In this model, analytics roles federate to domains (Data Analytic Lead → Data Analyst in domains). Business-proximity criteria become easy; centralized-consistency criteria degrade to medium or hard.

## Objects
- Model title: 3. Federated analytics; role note: Data Analytic Lead → Data Analyst (moved into domains)
- Rated easy (3/3, blue): close alignment with business needs; requests acceptance, avoiding bottleneck; time to market of projects; high context insights based on business knowledge
- Rated medium (2/3, amber): cost-effective balancing of teams capacity; effective analytics content and data governance; data security; unification of efforts, avoiding doublework
- Rated hard (1/3, pink): people development, retention and rotation; one-version-of-truth / cross-role usage; cross-functional reporting; solid navigation and UX; high content and process quality, standardisation
