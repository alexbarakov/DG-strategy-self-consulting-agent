## Summary
A filled example of a domain/subdomain map: six business domains as large color-coded hexagons, each surrounded by color-matched sticky notes naming its subdomains, with dotted curved connectors showing cross-domain relationships (e.g., Sales-Operations via Client Master Data, Finance-Sales, HR-Services Delivery). Five small gray placeholder hexagons at the periphery signal expandability. Companion example for the blank template in frame 3458764611453561760.

## Objects
- Sales (yellow): Sales Forecast, Demand, Client Master Data, Sales Managers Performance, ...
- Finance (blue): Financial Planning, Financial Forecast, Revenue Recognition, Expenses Analysis, Financial Unit Structure
- Operations (red): Procurement, Invoicing, Contracting, Collection, ...
- Marketing (gray): Client Communications, External Communications, ...
- Human Resources (purple): Employee Data, Compensation and Benefits, Recruitment Processing, Learning and Development, ...
- Services Delivery (orange): Delivery Quality, Customer Development, Project Delivery, Account Management
- Five small unlabeled gray hexagons (future/placeholder domains)
- ~10 dotted connectors marking cross-domain subdomain relationships
