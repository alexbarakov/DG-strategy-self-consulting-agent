## Summary
Findings on decentralized DG roles. Data Custodian (aka domain curator / data partner) is the most implemented role (65% of companies), usually filled by systems analysts or data engineers, responsible for DQ checks, architectural unification, and model documentation. Data Object Owner is less common and often compensated by a strong custodian or duty rotation. Business Data Steward is the most challenging role — engagement and consistent effectiveness are rare. DG committees/councils are largely absent, with DG discussions dispersed across other meetings; the author's editorial note argues dedicated DG meetings are essential if DG is to be established.

## Objects
- Data Custodian role — 65% adoption; systems analysts / data engineers
- Data Object Owner role — rare, compensated via custodian or duty rotation
- Business Data Steward role — hardest (glossaries, semantics, documentation, content)
- Committees and councils — largely absent; editorial: dedicated meetings are essential
- Implicit difficulty hierarchy: custodian < object owner < steward
