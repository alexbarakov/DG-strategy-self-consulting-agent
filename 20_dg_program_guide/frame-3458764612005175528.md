## Summary
A decision-support matrix comparing five operational models for data analytics teams across ~13 difficulty criteria grouped into Process Efficiency, User Experience, and Business Value, plus an Implementation Complexity row and company-profile fit ratings. Implementation complexity per model: Complete Centralization = Low; Hybrid Centralization with Matrix Management = High; Hybrid Federation with Core Team = High; Partial Federation (central data engineering) = Medium; Full Federation / Data Mesh = Medium. Company-profile fit is rated with an emoji scale for four archetypes (large enterprise, SME, digital product company with 100+ data professionals, digital product startup). Sourced from data-nature.com.

## Objects
- Five model columns: 1) Complete Centralization; 2) Hybrid Centralization with Matrix Management; 3) Hybrid Federation with a Core Team / Cross-Functional Analytics; 4) Partial Federation — Data Engineering Remains Centralized; 5) Full Federation (aka Data Mesh)
- Criteria framing row: "How difficult is it to ensure:"
- Process Efficiency criteria: cost-effective team capacity balancing; efficient data/content management; data security; cross-functional reporting; high-quality content/standardization; staff development, retention, rotation; avoiding duplicate work
- User Experience criteria: one version of the truth (cross-role usage); navigation and user experience
- Business Value criteria: alignment with business needs / closeness to the business; time-to-market; handling requests / avoiding bottlenecks; high-context insights based on business expertise
- Implementation Complexity ratings: Low / High / High / Medium / Medium
- Company profile fit rows with emoji scale (best to worst fit): Large Enterprise (medium-high complexity landscape); SME (low complexity landscape); Digital Product Company (complex landscape, 100+ data professionals); Digital Product Startup
