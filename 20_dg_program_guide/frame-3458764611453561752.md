## Summary
A workshop facilitation template for structured identification of data/analytics pain points across organizational roles. Goals: identify and analyze data issues in the organization, let everyone speak up about their pains, and focus solution design on specific problems (framed as preferable to an economic-model debate with management). The canvas is a grid of color-coded, role-specific pain zones around a central "Cross-Roles Pains" column; all zones are blank, awaiting participant sticky notes.

## Objects
- Goal block with three workshop objectives
- Pain zone: Pains of the entire company (yellow)
- Pain zone: Pains of "casual" users (pink)
- Pain zone: Pains of top management (blue)
- Pain zone: Pains of the BI team (orange)
- Pain zone: Pains of Power Users — advanced users / analysts (purple)
- Pain zone: Pains of the data platform / DWH team (blue)
- Central vertical "Cross-Roles Pains" column
- Two reference/example images (example filled boards)
- Purple left panel with title, goal, "Back to the board" navigation
