## Summary
An embedded full-length document: a customizable business case and executive guide for establishing a formal healthcare data governance program, attributed to Jeff Fuller, VP of Analytics at Divurgent. Part One is educational/persuasive (definition of DG, its role in analytics and AI, executive talking points, common use cases, barriers to adoption); Part Two is a fill-in business case template with a 10-section structure, a 3-phase implementation plan (Foundation, Launch, Engagement), resourcing guidance (1-3 FTE DG Office, start with existing tools like SharePoint/Drive before buying a catalog), and an illustrative cost-benefit framework. Frames DG as continuous organizational capability, not a one-time cleanup, and as the prerequisite for reliable AI training data.

## Objects
- Frame labeled "Case of" containing the document, 3 images, an embedded web/video element, and a connector
- Definition of DG: asset-management business function covering access, sharing, use, operating models, certification, inventories, quality, security, decision rights, accountability, definitions
- Role in analytics/AI: accuracy, consistency, reliability, transparency; AI cannot distinguish high-quality from poor-quality training data on its own
- Executive talking points: DG is both technology and business responsibility; pains — inaccessible/inconsistent data, unclear accountability, time wasted locating and preparing data
- Business problems/use cases: knowledge management, limited analytics capacity vs demand, regulatory/data-sharing risk, duplicate EHR patient records (Universal Master Patient Index), unused data, AI readiness
- Reasons organizations delay DG: no time/staff, "cheaper on demand" belief, waiting for a major event, political exposure, perceived difficulty/cost
- Program components: data needs and objectives; reference/master data; standardization; metadata, catalogs, definitions, lineage; DQ monitoring, incident management, alerts; policies (access, usage, security, lifecycle); stewardship, decision rights, roles; analytics governance, certified solutions, demand management, ROI
- Implementation phases: I Foundation (assess, define future state, roles/operating model, workstreams, comms); II Launch (initial domains, roadmaps, DG Office and Council, stewards, policies, baseline maturity); III Engagement (stewardship launch, formalized roles/KPIs, integration into SDLC, expanded services, education)
- Resources: DG Office ~1-3 FTE; existing stewards, council members, SMEs; existing collaboration tools first, licensed catalog/metadata tools later
- Cost-benefit example: tech ~$2-6K; personnel ~$115-250K (1-3 FTE); advisory ~$200-400K (4-8 months); ROI incl. >50% less time locating/characterizing data
- Business case template sections: organization/stakeholders, objective, current state, business drivers, program components, benefits, 3-phase plan, personnel/tech/training, cost-benefit/ROI, conclusion and approval request
- Known typos in source: "principals", "articles", "exiting", "HIPPA"
