## Summary
A worked Data Mesh Canvas example mapping data products across the three data-mesh layers: source-aligned (bottom), aggregate (middle), consumer-aligned (top). Eleven data products are placed under their owning domains, with 15 dashed directional connectors showing consumption flow upward — e.g., CRM's "360° Customer" aggregates multiple source products; Controlling's "Profitability Reporting" draws from both aggregate and source layers. Based on the datamesh-architecture.com canvas.

## Objects
- Source-aligned products: Search Queries (Product Search), Articles (Article Details), Orders + Customers (Checkout), Shipment (Fulfillment), Invoice (Payment)
- Aggregate products: ML Model (Recommendations), 360° Customer (CRM)
- Consumer-aligned products: Funnel Analytics (Marketing), KPIs & Dashboard (Management Support), Profitability Reporting (Controlling Support)
- 15 dashed connectors encoding cross-domain data product consumption dependencies
- Rotated layer labels: Source-aligned / Aggregate / Consumer-aligned
