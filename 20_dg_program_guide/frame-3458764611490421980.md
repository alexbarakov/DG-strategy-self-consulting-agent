### Card descriptions carried on the nodes
- **Data Issues** — "a non-fulfilment of a requirement regarding data quality. Generally a data issue concerns an incorrect data value, but it can be applied to any other data quality dimension, e.g. completeness and timeliness." Synonyms listed: data anomalies, data error, data quality incident, defect, error, nonconformity. Source link: datamanagement.wiki/data_quality_management_system/data_issue.
- **Data Lineage** — "metadata that identifies the sources of data and the transformations through which it has passed up to the point of consumption (DAMA-NL, 2020)".
- **Defining Metadata** — "Metadata is data that defines and describes the characteristics of other data (DAMA Dictionary). Defining metadata: metadata that enables interpretation of the meaning of data to provide information."
- **Critical Data Elements** — "a data element that is determined to be vital to the successful operation of the organization." Notes: examples are elements representing protected personal information, elements used in financial reports (internal and external), regulatory reports, identifying information of master data, elements critical for a decision-making process, elements used for measuring organizational performance. The Factor Rating Method is the method for deciding between two or more data elements; the Factor Rating Matrix is its output. Purpose: "to prioritize efforts to improve and ensure the quality of the most valuable data in the organization."
- **Data Quality Monitoring** — "the process of detecting and reporting data issues."
- **Roles and Responsibilities** — "Role is the actions and activities assigned to or required or expected of a person or group. Responsibility is the obligation to carry forward an assigned task to a successful conclusion." A typical data quality team includes: data owners, data stewards, data consumers, data producers, data analysts, data custodian.
- **Data Quality Policy** — "a policy with regard to data quality". Contains: importance of data quality; purpose of the policy; scope of the policy; relationship with the strategic objectives of the organisation; applicable laws, regulations and company decisions; critical data and priority DQ dimensions; policy statements about areas that contribute to data quality (people, processes, IT, agreements, documentation); roles and responsibilities about data quality; procedure to monitor the policy; procedure to evaluate and adjust the policy. Additional content: historical context, roadmap for implementation, references, definitions of terms, document version, management that established the latest version.
- **Data Delivery Agreement** — "In case of multiple data deliveries, the conditions for the delivery are recorded in a Data Delivery Agreement (DDA)" and include: data to be supplied (possibly specified in an appendix by source fields); classification of the data; data security; purpose of delivery (legal basis); use — whether or not forwarding is allowed; quality of the data; method of delivery (possibly technical specifications); time(s) of delivery; possibly costs; contact persons (source holder and customer); if applicable, ownership of the interfaces. Note: "DDA is also called Data Sharing Agreement."
- **Data Suppliers** — description is only a link to datamanagement.wiki/data_quality_management_system/data_supplier.
- **Data Profiling**, **Data Cleansing**, **Data Quality Rules**, **Data Quality Dimensions**, **Incident Tracking System**, **Data Quality**, **Data Consumers**, **Business rules**, **Databases and applications**, **Stakeholder analysis**, **Data quality objectives** — title only, no description text on the card.
