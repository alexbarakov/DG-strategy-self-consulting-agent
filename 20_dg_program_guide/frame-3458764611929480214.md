## Summary
Scorecard sub-frame accompanying the "4. Fully Federated (aka data mesh)" operating model (part of the Data Teams Modeling template, frame 3458764611929480210; the model title text sits in the parent frame). Same "How easy is to provide:" criteria on a 3-dot scale. Business-proximity criteria are easy; nearly all centralization-consistency criteria are hard.

## Objects
- Rated easy (3/3, blue): close alignment with business needs; requests acceptance, avoiding bottleneck; time to market of projects; high context insights based on business knowledge
- Rated hard (1/3, pink): people development, retention and rotation; cost-effective balancing of teams capacity; effective analytics content and data governance; data security; one-version-of-truth / cross-role usage; cross-functional reporting; solid navigation and UX; high content and process quality, standardisation; unification of efforts, avoiding doublework
