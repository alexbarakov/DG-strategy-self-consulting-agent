## Summary
A concept map tracing how data catalogs evolved through three generations, driven by five trends, converging into "Data Catalog 3.0". The Data Catalog 3.0 node connects (dotted arrows) to its defining concepts — active metadata approach (visually emphasized with the thickest arrow), a new decentralized/community-led vision for data governance, and the metadata lake — and outputs downstream "AI driven features" and 3rd-gen programmable bots (e.g. Slack-integrated bots for column descriptions and risk reduction).
