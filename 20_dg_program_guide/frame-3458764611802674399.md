## Summary
Evidence of DG efficiency outcomes ("entropy has been reduced with respect to data"). Concrete wins: deleting a large volume of data saved 20% of costs on a 4 PB Hadoop cluster; a data catalog improved TTM and incident resolution, proven by comparing chat-based vs catalog-based searches (number of queries, number of analysts, total analyst cost) — results justified further investment. Cautionary stat: in 30% of companies DG activities are being scaled back due to prolonged effort without clear results; the highlighted insight is that DG MVPs led by an experienced, respected senior lead reach tangible benefits faster.

## Objects
- Cost win: 20% saving on a 4 PB Hadoop cluster via data deletion
- Catalog ROI method: chat search vs catalog search comparison (queries, analysts, analyst cost)
- Stat: 30% of companies scaling back DG
- Insight: respected senior lead of the DG MVP as the key success factor
