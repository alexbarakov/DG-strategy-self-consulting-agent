## Summary
A slide titled "The number of 'complex' questions, for which vendors have no clear answers, is also increasing." Six provocative open questions about data catalog adoption, framed as sticky notes — good discussion prompts for a catalog initiative review.

## Objects
- Should we involve the business in working with the catalog, and if so, what should we show them so they don't run away in horror?
- Lineage — how do we stop the endless integrations and figure out who actually needs it and why?
- The report catalog duplicates your analytical portal, which is convenient for users. What should we do about this?
- What to do with the barren wasteland of field descriptions, and how to cultivate oases there? Are AI-generated descriptions useful to anyone?
- How do we measure the savings in data search and understanding (as vendors claim) when no one actually has time tracking for this?
- Do you have sufficient overall maturity to launch a data catalog and derive value from it?
