## Summary
An interactive "Data Governance Scope Configurator": a mapping canvas connecting DG Goals → DG Initiatives (streams) → Success factors → DG Value drivers. Instructions: define the goals most important for your company, remove irrelevant ones, check the linked DG streams that help achieve those goals, identify success factors, and remove the rest. Initiatives are grouped into named DG streams (shapes) with sticky-note sub-initiatives; a separate column lists the most common success factors.

## Objects
- **Data Governance Goals** (stickies): increasing trust in data and data products; speed up hiring/onboarding for data roles and reduce data-team turnover; increase speed of launching new business products/services; increase reuse of data objects and reporting capabilities; reduce lead time for data discovery; make data quality status transparent for the most important objects; minimize duplicate effort in data preparation/cleaning; accelerate integration of acquired companies; optimize resources for storing/maintaining DWH objects; eliminate inconsistencies in interpretation and values of business metrics; achieve desired and stable quality levels for the most important data; standardized operations in the data lifecycle; reducing costs; reducing time-to-market for data analytics projects; reduce compliance and security risks; effective notification of data consumers about DQ status/issues; predictable and rapid resolution of DQ tasks for critical objects; personal/critical data labeled and managed in specialized processes; increase quantity/quality/depth of penetration of data & analytics products into the business; increasing overall utility of data and analytics for the business
- **DG Initiative streams** (shapes with sticky sub-items):
  - Data quality management system — automation and monitoring of DG metrics; DQ checks coverage and processing flow; data contracts; effective consumer notification
  - Metadata management — data cataloging, lineage, Business Glossary, documentation, "metadata is clear and discoverable"
  - Data security and Compliance — automated access management and approval flows; role-based data access matrix; access audit synced with PII classification
  - Data Classification — criticality labeling; sensitivity/PII detection and labeling; data prioritisation logic
  - Data Platform Development — DevOps; change release impact assessment; unified data tooling (Golden Path); data platform medallion layers standard; usage statistics by systems/objects
  - Roles model and operating framework — data stewardship and custodianship; data ownership; DG/CDO office, DG Committee; federated operating model; data domain structure
  - Data standards — data lifecycle standards as code; DWH naming convention; Data Architecture Standards; business ontology
  - Building the golden layer — data products; MDM; layer of trusted certified data sources
  - Data Culture Development — data literacy projects; DWH School and standardized hiring
  - Analytics Governance — BI content certification and health assessment; BI Ops; metric store and unified semantic layer as code; centralized and self-service content transition flow
- **Most common Success factors** (stickies): responsibility assigned and taken for all DG activities; executive sponsorship; engaging experts from business; business use cases registry; service SLAs
- "Data Nature" label; several placeholder "..." stickies
