## Summary
A staged roadmap flowchart "Reasonable way to start Data Governance": from "Natural data governance" (no formal DG) through "Common Sense DG" on team level, then a DG MVP focused on the most painful areas, and finally "Land and expand" with a full DG program. Decision gates include the question "Do we need a formal/dedicated data governance?", required sponsorship levels (C-level support 1/4 → 2/4), MVP case packaging, budget approval vs budget denial (which loops back to continuing Common Sense DG and restarting the MVP). A legend classifies initiatives into Stage 1 "Essential Minimum", Stage 2 "Reasonable Addition", Stage 3 "Matured Focus", plus markers for leading/secondary role, tooling and initiative effort. Attributed to data-nature.com.

## Objects
- **Start / Natural data governance:** triggers are business drivers, pain points, visible incidents; question "Do we need a formal/dedicated data governance?"
- **MVP team formation:** create an MVP DG project team of 2-3 leads/senior members from DWH/BI teams with a systemic mindset and 2+ years in the company; requires support from CDO/CTO/CFO/CEO (min 1 of 4) and from BI, ML, DWH, Product Analytics team leaders; analyze pain points and DG drivers incl. their scale, review solution ideas and industry best practices
- **Common Sense DG (team level) example projects:** "clean-up" of the data and analytics platform based on usage statistics + simple monitoring; simple DQ monitoring (basic null checks, nightly updates); data access rules; git-driven documentation of the core data model
- **DG MVP** — simplified initiative on the most painful areas; choose the most critical business domain or a key data object (e.g., customer). Projects: domain metrics tree and glossary; documentation according to criticality; assigning owners/custodians/stewards to objects, testing responsibilities; setting up DQ checks, handling incidents, measuring data quality; data contracts with systems teams; creation and promotion of a "golden" layer of sources; (grey example card) creation of the semantic layer and metric store
- **Gate to program:** packaging MVP results in a case; interest from other business domain owners; support from CDO/CTO/CFO/CEO (min 2 of 4) + Business Domain Owner from the MVP; complete analysis of company data pain points → design of DG program concept → cost-benefit model → buy-in from sponsors. Outcomes: Budget approval → structuring and hiring DG office; Budget denial → ongoing development of Common Sense DG, restart MVP
- **Land and expand with a DG program** (program-level initiatives): design of domain structure; metrics tree and business ontology; classification of metrics and data objects by importance; development and maintenance of the glossary; classification of PII data; creation and development of the golden data layer; assigning owners and other roles to objects; documentation management based on criticality; standardization of data lifecycle operations (Data/BIOps); DQM coverage with checks based on criticality + public DQ status; incident management and DQ improvement; implementation of data contracts (embedded quality, documentation as code); creation of the semantic layer and metric store; automation of data/data-product access based on roles and domains; selection and development of tools (DQ tools, data catalog, dbt-like metric tools, data contract engines, observability); Architectural Committee / DG Committee; sponsorship management, case accumulation, promotion; aligning team priorities; reporting on DWH/BI, metric monitoring; "+100500 other good projects"
- **Legend:** Stage 1 "Essential Minimum" / Stage 2 "Reasonable Addition" / Stage 3 "Matured Focus" color codes; Leading Role / Secondary Role; Tooling; Initiative Effort
