## Summary
Title: **"Reasonable way to start Data Governance 🤔"**, credited data-nature.com. A single left-to-right flowchart with four labelled zones — *Natural data governance* (grey label), *Common Sense DG on team level* (mint label), *DG MVP* (pink label), *Land and expand with a DG program* (lilac label). Two of the zones are drawn as dotted-outline boxes containing their example projects. 114 items, no images.

## Objects

### Zone 0 — "Natural data governance"
Left edge: **Start**, a small circle fed by the dotted note **"Business Drivers, Pain points, Visible Incidents"**, and the question **"Do we need a formal / dedicated data governance?"**.

### Flow from Start
Start → card **"To create an MVP DG project team: 2-3 leads/senior members from DWH/BI teams with a systemic mindset and 2+ years of experience within the company."** Two dotted preconditions attach to this card: **"Support from the CDO, CTO, CFO, CEO (Minimum 1 out of 4)"** and **"Support from BI, ML, DWH, Product Analytics teams leaders"**.
→ card **"Analyze the pain points and drivers of DG within the company, including their scale. Review solution ideas within the team and industry best practices."**
This card then splits: a dotted arrow up to the **Common Sense DG** track, and a solid arrow down to the **DG MVP** track.

### Zone 1 — "Common Sense DG on team level, examples of projects"
Entry card: *"'Common sense DG' involves small quick win projects that individual teams can implement as internal initiatives"*, which points into the dotted box holding four example cards plus an "Etc." marker:
- "Clean-up" of the data and analytics platform based on usage statistics, Simple monitoring tools
- Simple data quality monitoring system – basic checks for null values and nightly updates
- Data Access Rules
- Git-driven documentation of the core data model
The box as a whole then feeds the program-concept card (see gate below).

### Zone 2 — "DG MVP – Simplified initiative focused on the most 'painful' areas"
Entry card: *"Choose the most critical business domain or one of the key data objects (e.g., customer)."* → dotted box with seven cards:
- Development of a domain metrics tree and glossary (S1)
- Documentation according to criticality (S1)
- Assigning owners / data custodian / data steward to objects, testing responsibilities (S1)
- Setting up DQ checks, handling incidents, measuring data quality (S1)
- Creation of date [sic] contracts with systems teams (S2)
- Creation and promotion of a "golden" layer of sources (S2)
- Creation of the Semantic Layer and Metric Store (grey — the legend example card)
plus an "Etc." marker. The box feeds the program-concept card.

### The gate between MVP and program
Card: **"Complete analysis of the company's data pain points ⇨ design of the DG program concept ⇨ cost-benefit model ⇨ buy-in from sponsors."** Three dotted inputs chain into it: **"Support from the CDO, CTO, CFO, CEO (Minimum 2 out of 4)"** → **"Business Domain Owner from the MVP"** → **"Interest from Other Business Domain Owners"** → the card. To its left, the grey note **"Packaging results of MVP in a case"**.
Two outcomes leave the card:
- **Budget approval** (green) → **"Structuring and Hiring DG office"**, and on into the program zone.
- **Budget Denial** (crimson) → a crimson circle → crimson dashed arrow back into the Common Sense DG box, annotated **"Ongoing Development of Common Sense DG; Restart MVP"**.

### Zone 3 — "Land and expand with a DG program"
The gate card fans out to three Stage-1 cards: *Metrics Tree and Business Ontology*, *Design of Domain Structure*, *Standardization of Data Lifecycle Operations on the Platform (Data/BIOps)*. From there the graph continues exactly as on the sibling frame 3458764611802674402:
- Metrics Tree and Business Ontology → *Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance* (S1) and → *Development and Maintenance of the Glossary* (S3)
- Design of Domain Structure → *Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance* and → *Assigning Owners and Other Roles to Objects* (S1)
- Classification of Metrics and Data Objects by Importance → *Development and Maintenance of the Glossary* (S3), *Classification of PII Data* (S2), *Creation and Development of the Golden Data Layer* (S2)
- Creation and Development of the Golden Data Layer → *Creation of the Semantic Layer and Metric Store* (S3)
- Classification of PII Data → *Automation of Data and Data Product Access Based on Roles and Domains* (S3)
- Assigning Owners and Other Roles to Objects → *Setting Up Documentation Management Based on Criticality* (S1) and → *DQM – Coverage with Checks Based on Criticality / Public DQ Status* (S1)
- Setting Up Documentation Management Based on Criticality → *Implementation of Data Contracts: Embedded Quality and Documentation as Code* (S2)
- Standardization of Data Lifecycle Operations → *DQM – Coverage with Checks Based on Criticality*
- DQM – Coverage with Checks ↔ *Incident Management and Data Quality Improvement* (S1), which also → *Implementation of Data Contracts* (S2)
Bottom row, outside the chain, under the label **"+100500 of other good projects"**: *Reporting on DWH/BI, Metric Monitoring* (S1); *Aligning Team Priorities* (S1); *Architectural Committee, DG Committee* (S2); *Sponsorship management, accumulation of cases, promotion* (S2); *Selection and Development of Tools: DQ tools, Data catalog tools, dbt-like metric tools, data contracts engines, data observability tools, etc.* (S3).
