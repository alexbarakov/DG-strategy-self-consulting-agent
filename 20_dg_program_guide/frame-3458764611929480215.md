## Summary
Scorecard sub-frame for the "1. Fully Centralized" data team operating model (part of the Data Teams Modeling template, frame 3458764611929480210). Rates "How easy is to provide:" a fixed set of criteria on a 3-dot scale (blue = easy, amber = medium, pink = hard). Centralization benefits score high; business-proximity criteria score low.

## Objects
- Model title: 1. Fully Centralized; roles shown: Data Governance Lead → Domain Custodian / Curator
- Rated easy (3/3, blue): cost-effective balancing of teams capacity; effective analytics content and data governance; data security; one-version-of-truth / cross-role usage; cross-functional reporting; solid navigation and user experience; high content and processes quality, standardisation; unification of efforts, avoiding doublework; people development, retention and rotation
- Rated hard (1/3, pink): close alignment with business needs; requests acceptance, avoiding bottleneck; time to market of projects
- Rated medium (2/3, amber): high context insights based on business knowledge
