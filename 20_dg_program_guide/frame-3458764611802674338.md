## Summary
Methodology and disclaimer slide for the DG Kitchen research. Scope: 20 tech companies from 5 countries, all sector leaders, 3,000–100,000+ employees, all with advanced data platforms; participants included a top-5 worldwide neobank, a top-3 classified platform, two top-10 delivery marketplaces, and a top-10 e-commerce platform. Companies stay anonymous to enable honest pattern analysis; the goal is common failures and good practices, not individual stories. Explicit warning: described approaches may reflect either effective solutions or immaturity — apply critical thinking, not "best practice" labels.
