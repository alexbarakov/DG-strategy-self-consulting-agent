## Summary
A Data & Analytics operating model maturity matrix mapping organizational units (Finance Department, Service Lines, Business Lines) against 10 BI/data capability dimensions, with sticky-note statuses: green = done, blue = to be, yellow = in progress. It is a transformation-roadmap assessment tool distinguishing current from target capability adoption per business unit. In the sample state, Finance Department has confirmed Self-Service ETL, own governed data layer, and harmonized D&A landscape; most other rows are unassessed.

## Objects
- 10 capability columns: using central cross-functional BI reports/data products (consumers); centralized reporting factory service (customers); managing BI development in a matrix operating model; Self-Service BI; Self-Service ETL on core data layer; building own governed data layer; D&A landscape harmonized with the Core one (unified governance, entity reuse/dedup); DQ tooling for automated checks/monitoring; Data Catalog for domain assets; building and maintaining data products
- Rows: Finance Department, Service Lines 2-3+, Business Lines 1-3+
- Status legend: done (green) / to be (blue) / in progress (yellow)
- Pool of ~30 status sticky notes for placement
