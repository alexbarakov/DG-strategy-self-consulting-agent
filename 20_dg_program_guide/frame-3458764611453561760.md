## Summary
The blank template counterpart of the domain/subdomain map (see filled example in frame 3458764611453561759). Seven generic domains ("Domain 1"..."Domain 7", numbering skips 5) as color-coded hexagons, each with 3-4 empty color-matched sticky notes for subdomains, five small gray peripheral hexagons for future domains, and ~10 dotted connectors pre-drawn to illustrate cross-domain relationships. Intended for DDD-style / enterprise-architecture mapping of a company's own data domains.

## Objects
- Domain 1 (yellow), Domain 2 (red/pink), Domain 3 (purple), Domain 4 (orange), Domain 6 (gray), Domain 7 (blue) — large hexagons with empty subdomain stickies
- Five small gray peripheral hexagons (placeholder/satellite domains)
- 27 mostly-empty sticky notes color-matched to parent domain; two "..." overflow stickies
- 10 dotted cross-domain connector lines (e.g., Domain 1-Domain 2, Domain 1-Domain 7, Domain 3-Domain 6)
