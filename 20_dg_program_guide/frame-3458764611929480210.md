## Summary
A template with organizational prototypes for structuring data teams to balance centralized data governance and self-service analytics — "ensuring efficient collaboration, maximized time-to-market of projects and cost efficiency". It shows a series of org-chart prototypes progressing from centralized to "4. Fully Federated (aka data mesh)", each depicting the Data platform organization vs Business Domains 1-3, which roles sit where, which roles are "Managing" vs "Contributing", and which capabilities remain shared services. Variants covered include: data analytics delegated to business domains; both analytics and BI/reporting delegated; data engineering staying fully central ("more often case"); project management shifting to domain-driven; all streams distributed while keeping central-driven management. A blank area invites you to "Draw your company Model based on prototypes on the left".

## Objects
- Goal statement: organize data teams within an organization optimizing the balance between centralized data governance and self-service analytics
- Org prototypes (per option), with columns Data platform / Business Domain 1 / 2 / 3, roles → shared services split:
  - Roles used: CTO/CDO/CIO; CAO; CPO; BI Lead / Head of; Data Analytic Lead / Head of (→ Data Analyst); Data Engineering Lead (→ Data Platform Data Engineer); DS/ML Lead; Data Governance Lead; BI CoE / Community Leader; Data Analytic CoE Leader; BI / Analytics Lead (in-domain); Business Domain management; Product Owner / Product Manager (→ Business Analyst); Business Analyst / Business Data steward; BI Developer; Data Analyst; Data Engineer; Data Scientists / ML Engineers; Domain Custodian / Curator
  - Shared-service capability blocks: Infrastructure; Data analytics tools maintenance; Core Data Model / Architecture; Golden data layer / Data products; Glossary / Metric store; Metadata management / Cataloging data; Data Governance / Data Quality framework; Security / Access management
- Named variants: "Operating Models by domains"; Option 1 — Data analytics delegated to Business domains; Option 2 — Both Data analytics and BI/Reporting delegated to Business domains; Option 2 — Data engineering stays fully central (more often case); Option 3 — Project management shifts to domain-driven; Option 1 — All streams distributed with central-driven management kept; 4. Fully Federated (aka data mesh)
- Legend: "Managing:" vs "Contributing (in):"; roles → shared services
- Working area: "Draw your company Model based on prototypes on the left"; pointers "Place in the guide map", "Relevant links"
