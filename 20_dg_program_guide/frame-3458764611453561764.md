## Summary
A comprehensive template for authoring a Data Governance / Data & Analytics Vision Statement, illustrated with a sample bank-context vision (data monetization, governance structure, CDO leadership, key data assets, measurement frameworks — shown grayed out as placeholder). The main grid has three sections: Vision Statement, Strategic Goals + Strategic Initiatives (each with KPIs/Metrics, Business Outcomes, Timing columns), and Challenges & Risks (with Risk Reduction Factors). A right-hand guidance panel provides an 11-question checklist covering value extraction, critical assets, ownership, stakeholders, strategic alignment, services, objectives, products, KPIs, risks, and priority initiatives. Also available as an Excel/Google Sheets file via embedded link.

## Objects
- Goal: create a clear, inspiring vision defining future D&A direction, key initiatives, and core metrics
- Vision Statement field with grayed-out sample bank vision
- Strategic Goals table: Strategic Goals | KPIs/Metrics | Business Outcomes | Timing
- Strategic Initiatives table: same column structure
- Challenges and Risks table: Challenges/Risks | Risk Reduction Factors
- 11-point guidance checklist (right panel) with dotted connector to the vision area
- Six sticky notes, one flagging a real risk example: potential company acquisition leading to BI stack consolidation
- Excel/Google Sheets template hyperlink; purple sidebar with title, goal, "Back to the board"
