## Summary
A slide arguing that "The benefits of a data catalog are often lower than the costs of its creation and maintenance." The proposed response: investments can be reduced, value can be increased, or both. Advice: "Understand what drives value for you in a data catalog and enhance those aspects" — illustrated with three example value stickies.

## Objects
- Thesis: benefits of a data catalog are often lower than creation + maintenance costs
- Levers: reduce investments; increase value; do both
- Advice: understand what drives value for you in a data catalog and enhance those aspects
- Example value drivers (stickies): time spent searching for and understanding data objects decreased for data people = time-to-market for data projects improved; cross-utilization of data objects (incl. certified) increased; data and BI analysts onboarding faster
