## Objects

### Left half — challenges of "life without Data Governance", in starting it (5 statements)
1. "It's really hard to clearly connect DG to business goals, and sometimes it's impossible."
2. "The identified business value drivers are insufficient to get stakeholders to 'buy into' the project."
3. "There's too much reliance on business support. The business supports DG but will never delay its own tasks for it. The data/analytics leaders themselves are not yet ready to lead DG and the initiative lacks a clear owner."
4. "The complexity and lack of transparency in DG concepts — Data teams simply don't understand what it's all about."
5. "Domain teams are often unwilling to share data and collaborate."

### Right half — challenges of "life with Data Governance", in gaining value (9 statements)
Upper band:
1. "Starting DG as a project without adapting to business changes makes the program rigid and inflexible."
2. "Reproducing DG artifacts 'by the DMBOK' leads to a disconnect from the business. DG is isolated and detached from daily operations."
3. "Ignoring the involvement of employees who work with data every day. There's no real acceptance of roles, and no one is truly working on this."

Middle band:
4. "Failure to identify and focus on the most valuable data leads to wasted resources, time, and credibility."
5. "Getting stuck in complex initiatives lacking the push-mindset to make real progress."
6. "Product mindset that 'data isn't just yours' is not coming — domains don't take a proactive role."

Lower band:
7. "'We developed an approach, but They do not follow it.'"
8. "Too much reliance on tools — hiding behind data catalogs."
9. "The company culture rejects all bureaucracy-like initiatives."
