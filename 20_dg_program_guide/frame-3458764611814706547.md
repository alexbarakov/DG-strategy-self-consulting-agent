## Summary
A two-sided diagnostic canvas of DG friction points across the program lifecycle. Left side — challenges of "life without DG" (barriers to starting): hard to connect DG to business goals, insufficient value drivers for stakeholder buy-in, over-reliance on business support with no clear owner, opaque DG concepts, and domain teams unwilling to share data. Right side — challenges of "life with DG" (barriers to gaining value): rigid project framing, by-the-DMBOK artifact reproduction detached from operations, failure to focus on the most valuable data, getting stuck in complex initiatives, hiding behind data catalogs, ignoring everyday data workers, unadopted approaches, missing product mindset in domains, and cultural rejection of bureaucracy. Includes two DG memes.

## Objects
- Left panel: 5 "starting DG" challenges (business-goal linkage, weak value drivers, no owner / over-reliance on business, concept opacity, domain resistance)
- Right panel: 9 "gaining value" challenges (rigidity, DMBOK-copying, wrong data focus, stuck initiatives, tool over-reliance, ignored practitioners, "we developed an approach but they do not follow it", missing product mindset, anti-bureaucracy culture)
- Divider with bidirectional arrows separating the two lifecycle stages
- Memes: "thinking about vs actually starting a DG program"; "You can't buy Data Governance"
